# GRACE Supplemental Showcase

Selected qualitative examples at seed 7, matched by task and initial state. GRACE completes each selected task; at least one comparator fails or finishes in more execution steps. The main showcase covers ten tasks, with ten additional initializations in the supplemental collection. Aggregate performance is reported separately in the paper.
