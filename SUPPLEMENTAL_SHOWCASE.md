# Supplemental showcase: additional initializations

Selected examples of successful or earlier completion by CURVE. Each comparison shows all eight methods on the same task and initial state.

Completion is measured in recorded execution steps. All panels advance together at 24 fps, one frame per step; successful terminal frames are held. These are execution-step comparisons, not measurements of inference latency.

## 1. turn on the stove and put the moka pot on it

Task 2 / init 24. [Watch the eight-method comparison](comparison_videos/task02_init24_all_methods.mp4).

CURVE succeeds; POTR does not complete the task.

CURVE completes in 229 steps, earlier than Native (235 steps); SEAM (297 steps); RTC (306 steps); BID-Backward (382 steps); DVAC (233 steps); PACE (256 steps).
