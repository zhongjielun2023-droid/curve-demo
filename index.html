THE IDEA

History becomes geometry.

The past shapes how a chunk is generated.
The present determines what is generated.

Read the shape of the past.

Completed action generations carry directional structure. CURVE distills their bending into a geometric memory.

Shape the next generation.

This memory shapes the next action chunk while the current observation guides what the robot should do.

Act, observe, replan.

Execute part of the chunk, observe the scene again, and repeat. Each new generation responds to the latest feedback.

A training-free approach to action-chunk replanning, demonstrated with OpenPI π₀.₅ on LIBERO-10.
