# Between Glances — GRACE in action

**GRACE — Geometric Replanning with Adaptive Chunk Execution**

A robot reaches, grasps, and carries on between observations. Geometric memory guides
how the movement continues, while stability determines when the robot looks again.

Open [the demonstration page](index.html) to watch three featured movements,
explore [ten different tasks](MAIN_SHOWCASE.md), or browse [more examples](SUPPLEMENTAL_SHOWCASE.md).

Each example includes an eight-method comparison and individual videos for Native,
SEAM, RTC, POTR, BID-Backward, DVAC, PACE, and GRACE. Use **See GRACE finish** to jump
to the completed task, or follow a method on its own. The outcomes panel shows which
methods succeed and how many steps they take to finish.

Choose a task, play the movement, and compare the results. Links preserve the selected
example and method, and every video can be downloaded from its player.
